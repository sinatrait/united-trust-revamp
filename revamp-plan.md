# The United Trust homepage: review and revamp plan

**Reviewed:** 22 October 2026  
**Source:** [theunitedtrust.in](https://theunitedtrust.in/)

## Screenshots

Homepage screenshots were captured in the browser during this review: the hero/header, the About section, and the Services section. They are included with the browser captures in this chat; no image files were added to the project.

## Current visual system

### Colors

| --- | --- |
| Page/header background | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Main text | Charcoal `#3D3C3C` |
| Dark content sections | Near-black `#222222` |
| About/section background | Very pale lavender `#F9F9FF` |
| Message section | Bright green `#009F4D` |

The current palette mixes several unrelated accent colors. The logo's orange and green are distinctive, but the blue links, teal, bright-green message band, lavender panels, and dark project area do not yet feel like one system.

### Typography

- Page text renders in **Poppins, sans-serif**.
- The measured body style is 14px, weight 300, with a 22.75px line height.
- The desktop hero heading is 72px, weight 700, white, and uppercase.
- The logo is an image containing its own display lettering and script tagline; its exact typefaces cannot be identified from the rendered page.

## Homepage structure

1. **Header/navigation:** White header with the logo/tagline and links to Home, About, What We Do, Activities, and Contact Us. At the inspected narrow viewport, navigation is represented by a hamburger menu.
2. **Hero:** Full-width photo composite with a strong dark overlay, “Welcome to United Trust” headline, three short statements, and a prominent “Please Contact us to donate” button.
3. **About Us:** Introductory organization description on a pale background.
4. **Vision / Mission:** Two adjacent text panels beneath the About introduction, each with a heading and paragraph.
5. **Services / What We Do:** Introductory paragraph followed by nine image-and-text initiatives: education, healthcare, convention center, affordable housing, library, divine research center, administration, weddings, and food program. The section uses a dark background and alternating image/text presentation.
6. **Volunteers:** Introductory copy and groups of trustees, legal advisers, and volunteers.
7. **Our Message:** A prominent green section with a message and brochure link.
8. **Contact Us / footer:** Contact summary and links.

**Blog and Testimonials:** Neither appears as a homepage section in the inspected page. The existing navigation also has no Blog or Testimonials link.

## What to modernize

- **Clarify the first impression:** Replace the busy, layered hero image with one strong, authentic community photograph. Keep the headline concise and add clear, separate actions for donating and learning about the trust.
- **Improve navigation and accessibility:** Make the desktop navigation visible and easy to scan; use a clear mobile menu control, consistent active states, descriptive image text, and strong keyboard focus and contrast.
- **Unify the visual system:** Build a restrained palette around the logo's green and warm coral/orange, with neutral backgrounds and one dark text color. Reserve accents for important links and actions instead of assigning different colors to unrelated sections.
- **Establish typography hierarchy:** Retain Poppins if it suits the identity, but define a consistent responsive scale, more readable body sizing, and sentence-case headings. Avoid relying on oversized all-caps text for section hierarchy.
- **Make the story easier to follow:** Present About, Vision, and Mission as a compact, coherent introduction; use consistent spacing and a clear heading structure.
- **Make services scannable:** Replace the long alternating dark rows with a responsive set of clearly labeled initiative summaries. Give images useful alternative text and use links only where a visitor can take a meaningful next step.
- **Strengthen trust and impact:** Show current, verifiable outcomes or project updates with dates and context. Add Testimonials only when consented, attributable quotes are available; add a Blog only if it can be kept current.
- **Make donation and contact paths concrete:** Give donation and contact actions clear destinations and make phone/email details easy to use on mobile.
- **Review consistency and content polish:** Standardize capitalization, spelling, labels, image treatment, and link styles across sections. Check all contact details, destinations, and claims before publication.

## Suggested direction

Keep the trust's recognizable logo and community-first message, but pair them with a calmer, more consistent palette; authentic photography; clearer calls to action; more accessible navigation; and a shorter, more scannable homepage. Treat Blog and Testimonials as optional additions, not assumed existing content.
